# 01: Product & Business Strategy

This document defines the "Why" behind SchemaForge—its target market, the problem it solves, and the refined "Founder Edition" monetization strategy.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Existing database visualizers (like ChartDB) often suffer from:
- **Cluttered UX**: Interfaces designed for 2010-era aesthetics.
- **AI as an Afterthought**: Chatbots bolted onto sidebars rather than woven into the canvas.
- **High Friction**: Mandatory logins and complex setup just to sketch a schema.

**SchemaForge** solves this by prioritizing **Design-Led Engineering (DX)**. It is a canvas-first, AI-integrated architect's tool.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary**: Backend engineers designing new microservices or migrating existing databases.
- **Secondary**: Tech Leads / Architects reviewing schemas in pull requests.
- **Tertiary**: Product Managers who need a "read-only" visual of data structures.

## 3. Monetization: The "Founder Edition" Model
We have refined a three-tier strategy that balances business sustainability with developer trust.

### Tier 1: Free (The "Hook")
*   **Limit**: 3 Active Projects / 15 Tables per project.
*   **AI**: Basic generation models (Throttled).
*   **Exports**: Standard SQL (Postgres, MySQL).
*   **Goal**: Frictionless entry for hobbyists and students.

### Tier 2: Pro ($19/mo - The "Power Builder")
*   **Limit**: Unlimited Projects & Tables.
*   **AI**: Priority access to high-end models (Managed by SchemaForge).
*   **Exports**: Advanced ORM formats (Prisma, Drizzle, DBML).
*   **Features**: Real-time collaboration via shareable links.
*   **Goal**: Professional developers and small teams.

### Tier 3: Lifetime ("Founder Edition")
*   **Price**: $199 one-time payment.
*   **Scarcity**: Limited to the first **1,000 users**.
*   **Perks**: 
    - All Pro features forever.
    - Exclusive "Obsidian" Premium Theme.
    - "Founder" Badge on shared schemas.
*   **AI Strategy**: **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)**. 
    - Users can plug in their own OpenAI/Anthropic/Google API keys.
    - This removes the recurring cost for SchemaForge while giving users 100% control over their AI spend.
*   **Optional**: A "Prepaid AI Credit" system for those who want managed AI without a subscription.

## 4. Competitor Analysis & Market Position
- **Prisma/Drizzle**: They provide ORM tools but lack the "Whiteboard" design experience.
- **ChartDB/dbdiagram.io**: Good tools, but dated UI/UX.
- **SchemaForge Positioning**: The "Linear of Database Design." High-speed, high-aesthetics, and AI-native.

## 5. Marketing & SEO
- **Keywords**: "Visual Database Designer", "AI Schema Generator", "SQL to ERD", "Prisma Schema Visualizer".
- **Strategy**: 
    - **Community-Led**: Sharing beautiful "Obsidian" schemas on Twitter/X and LinkedIn.
    - **Developer Utility**: The free "SQL to Diagram" tool acts as a high-conversion funnel.
